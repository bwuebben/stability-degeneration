\begin{abstract}
We construct local K\"ahler potentials for the $L^2$ metric on moduli
spaces of Hermitian--Yang--Mills connections over polarized projective
surfaces that extend continuously to ideal connections.
The potential combines a zeta determinant on a curve avoiding the bubble
points with a weighted curvature integral and an explicit term for the
defect cycle. On a projective K3 surface with a generic polarization and its Ricci-flat
metric, let the Mukai vector be primitive, of rank at least two and
positive square, with nonempty stable bundle locus. The potential identifies the $L^2$ metric with the canonical singular
Ricci-flat metric on the normalized Uhlenbeck compactification.
Its completion is the Uhlenbeck compactification, and in complex
dimension $2N$ its volume is $(16\pi^4\Vol X)^N/N!$.
In rank two, the strata with stable residual bundle and distinct simple
bubble points are totally geodesic hyperk\"ahler orbifold strata, with
the base-surface metric scaled by $4\pi^2$.
At stable-residual boundary points, we identify the affine tangent cones
and their Reeb actions and compute their volume densities from instanton
partition functions.
\end{abstract}
