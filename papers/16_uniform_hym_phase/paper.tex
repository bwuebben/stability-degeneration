\begin{abstract}
Near a fixed locally free polystable bundle on a compact K\"ahler manifold,
we construct an exact finite-dimensional invariant reduction of
Hermitian--Yang--Mills flow with differentiable asymptotic phase.
If the harmonic deformation coefficients remain in a prescribed
neighborhood and the initial metric has sufficiently small normal
displacement, a selected reduced trajectory reconstructs an exact flow on
the same holomorphic bundle. The relative metric error decays exponentially
in a fixed H\"older norm, with constants controlled by the complementary
elliptic gap and independent of the smallest positive finite-dimensional eigenvalues, their ratios,
and stabilizer changes. The phase depends continuously differentiably on
the initial metric, even when the reconstructing complex gauges are unbounded.
The construction retains arbitrary multiplicities and the complete
convergent Kuranishi obstruction. It combines exact noncommuting metric
coordinates with a parameter-dependent invariant graph and a group-valued
phase estimate. An obstructed repeated-factor bundle on a smooth bicubic
threefold supplies noncommuting integrable coefficients with explicitly
separated finite spectral scales.
\end{abstract}
