\begin{abstract}
We give a terminating construction of the marked integral
vanishing-cycle map for a six-parameter family of Calabi--Yau
conifold smoothings in $\mathbb P^3\times\mathbb P^3$.
The eight vanishing spheres span a primitive rank-six lattice
whose symplectic normal form is determined by topology.
We recover their coordinates in an independent geometric marking
by constructing an effective neighbourhood in vanishing-period
coordinates and absolute error bounds for cycle transport and
integration. Picard--Lefschetz period jumps determine the integral
columns and monodromy without an a priori bound on the integer
entries or restrictions on nonzero period ratios. Recovery at one
reference fibre suffices throughout the smooth locus.
As applications, we verify the hypotheses of established analytic
results on the same fibres in a possibly ineffective smaller
neighbourhood. Over $\mathbb R$, the kernel of the recovered map
is the image of divisor restriction to the exceptional curves;
its capacity-weighted quadratic form and limiting harmonic mass
give relative asymptotics for the two small positive Ricci-flat
two-form eigenvalues. For the constructed nonflat polystable
rank-four bundles, a local invariant manifold of
Hermitian--Yang--Mills metric flow is tangent at the
Hermitian--Einstein metric to the complete low spectral subspace
of the Laplacian on trace-free Hermitian endomorphisms,
including its kernel.
\end{abstract}
