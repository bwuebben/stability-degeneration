\begin{abstract}
We give a terminating construction of the marked integral
vanishing-cycle homomorphism for a six-parameter family of
Calabi--Yau conifold smoothings in
$\mathbb P^3\times\mathbb P^3$. Its eight local spheres span
a primitive rank-six lattice in free middle homology of rank $134$.
Integral topology determines its symplectic normal form and
torsion-free quotient. The period construction recovers its
coordinates in an independently constructed geometric marking.
Starting from algebraically selected equations, we construct
a known neighbourhood in vanishing-period coordinates,
a geometric symplectic marking, and absolute error bounds
for transport and integration of represented cycles.
Picard--Lefschetz period jumps recover the integral columns
and monodromy without an a priori bound on the integral
coordinates or a restriction on nonzero period ratios.
The recovered matrix is constant in the transported marking,
so recovery at one reference fibre suffices throughout the
smooth family. On a possibly ineffective smaller neighbourhood,
we verify applicability of established Ricci-flat Hodge,
Hermitian--Yang--Mills flow and harmonic moduli results,
and identify the kernel of the recovered map with the
divisor restriction image governing the weak Hodge energy.
\end{abstract}
