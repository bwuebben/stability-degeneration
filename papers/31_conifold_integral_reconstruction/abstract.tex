\begin{abstract}
We give a terminating construction of the marked integral
vanishing-cycle homomorphism for a six-parameter family of
Calabi--Yau conifold smoothings in
$\mathbb P^3\times\mathbb P^3$. Its eight local spheres span
a rank-six lattice in free middle homology of rank $134$.
Starting from algebraically selected equations, we construct
a known neighbourhood in vanishing-period coordinates,
a geometric symplectic marking, and absolute error bounds
for transport and integration of represented cycles.
Picard--Lefschetz period jumps recover the integral columns,
their saturation and monodromy without a height prior or
a restriction on nonzero period ratios. We verify the
hypotheses of established analytic results on a possibly
ineffective smaller neighbourhood. There the capacity-weighted
squared norm of restriction of divisor classes from the
small resolution to its exceptional curves governs the two
small positive Ricci-flat two-form eigenvalues; the image
of this restriction is the kernel of the recovered
vanishing-cycle homomorphism. For nonflat bundles on the
same fibres, a finite-dimensional invariant manifold of
Hermitian--Yang--Mills metric flow is tangent at each
constructed Hermitian--Einstein metric to the full low
eigenspace on trace-free Hermitian endomorphisms.
\end{abstract}
