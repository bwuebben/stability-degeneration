\begin{abstract}
We determine the leading metric and concentration scales of
Hermitian--Yang--Mills flow for locally free extensions
\(0\to L\to V\to\mathcal I_WM\to0\) on a compact Kähler
surface, where \(L,M\) are distinct line bundles of equal
degree and \(W\) is a nonempty reduced finite scheme.
Fix Hermitian--Einstein metrics on the line factors and
their product as determinant. For every smooth initial
metric with this determinant, the integrated squared norm
\(Z(T)\) of the fixed inclusion satisfies
\(Z(T)\sim v_0^2/(4\pi^2\sigma T)\), where \(v_0\) is
the volume and
\(\sigma=\sum_{p\in W}(\det Q_p)^{-1/2}\);
\(Q_p\) is the quadratic form of the inclusion norm
at \(p\) in orthonormal coordinates.
On the radius
\(\{Z(T)/(v_0\sqrt{\det Q_p})\}^{1/2}\), the actual
metrics converge smoothly to an explicit charge-one
anti-self-dual metric. The inclusion half-height radius
and the half-defect-energy radius have this same leading
value. The proof corrects a compact family of metrics
orthogonally to its small connection eigenspace and
compares the resulting trajectory with every actual
flow. It also gives a global inclusion profile and
quantitative convergence outside shrinking balls.
For arbitrary finite defects we prove \(Z(T)=O(T^{-1})\);
if a defect is nonreduced, then \(TZ(T)\to0\).
\end{abstract}
