\begin{abstract}
We determine the small positive Hodge eigenvalues on two-forms in a
proper holomorphic family of compact threefolds over a disc, with smooth
K\"ahler total space, smooth fibres away from the origin, and a reduced
central fibre whose only singularities are ordinary double points. The
fibre metrics are induced from the total space. Assume that a small
resolution $Y$ of the central fibre has vanishing
first Betti number and second real cohomology spanned by first Chern
classes of holomorphic line bundles. The number of small eigenvalues is
the drop in the second Betti number from $Y$ to a smooth fibre. Each is
asymptotic to $2\pi\mu/\log(1/|t|)$, where $t$ is the family parameter.
The numbers $\mu$ are the relative eigenvalues of two quadratic forms on
$H^2(Y;\RR)$ modulo the classes that extend to the smooth fibres:
the sum of squared degrees on the exceptional curves, and the minimum
squared $L^2$ norm of a harmonic representative on the regular part of
the central fibre. We
express the latter form through divisor intersections and the K\"ahler
class. The remaining positive spectra in degrees one and two have a
uniform gap, and the small eigenforms are coexact, primitive and of type
$(1,1)$. The proof combines the norm calculation for a harmonic two-form
of unit period on the node link, a sharp leading energy bound on the
affine quadric smoothing, and the
compactness theorem of Ann\'e and Takahashi.
\end{abstract}
