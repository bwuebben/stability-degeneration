\begin{abstract}
We prove a finite-dimensional limit for Hermitian--Yang--Mills metric flow
on the trivial bundle over a compact curve approaching a reduced nodal
fibre. The total space is smooth and K\"ahler, the central components are
smooth, and each node joins distinct components. On the time scale
$\log(1/|t|)$, where $t$ is the degeneration parameter, the limiting
component metrics follow the gradient flow of the sum of their squared
symmetric-space distances, with component areas as masses and one coupling
for each node. For uniformly bounded determinant-one initial metrics with
prescribed local $L^2$ limits away from the nodes, we prove uniform global
$L^2$ tracking on bounded rescaled
time intervals and pointwise tracking away from the nodes after a fixed
physical-time transient. The construction combines component Green
functions, exact stationary neck metrics and the Donaldson trace inequality.
At the constant flat metric, we also identify the rescaled linearized heat
evolution on the small spectral subspace and prove a uniform gap on its
orthogonal complement. A rank-two example on a smoothing of a triangle of
projective lines has component metrics that admit no simultaneous
diagonalization by a fixed holomorphic frame.
\end{abstract}
