# L² Hodge theory and the LLV algebra of singular K3 moduli spaces

By [Bernd Johannes Wuebben](https://bwuebben.github.io/), [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0002-4588-3284).

Preprint. Original date: 14 November 2026. This version: 14 November 2026. 38 pages.

[Read the paper](l2-hodge-llv.pdf) · [Abstract and citation](https://bwuebben.github.io/papers/l2-hodge-llv/) · [Zenodo:24229948](https://zenodo.org/records/24229948) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24229948)

We identify the Looijenga–Lunts–Verbitsky algebra on the intersection cohomology of singular moduli spaces of sheaves on K3 surfaces with the orthogonal Lie algebra of their second cohomology enlarged by a hyperbolic plane. The surfaces have Picard rank one, the Mukai vector is twice a primitive vector of square at least four, and the rank and polarisation satisfy numerical conditions ensuring that all semistable sheaves are locally free. These moduli spaces have two singular strata and admit no symplectic resolution. For their natural hyperkähler metric, we identify square integrable cohomology with intersection cohomology and prove that harmonic forms are preserved by the three Lefschetz operators and their adjoints. The analytic argument extends the Kähler cone theorem to singular links and establishes invariance of operator domains under metric comparisons that distinguish tangential and normal directions at the strata. Monodromy then extends the relations supplied by one positive three-plane to the full second cohomology. We also determine the subrepresentation generated by the unit and, under an explicit Hodge-theoretic comparison hypothesis, the complete representation in dimension eighteen.

The main theorem retains the Picard-rank-one, twice-primitive and numerical locally free hypotheses. The complete representation in complex dimension eighteen is conditional on the Hodge-structure comparison (H3); the character calculation alone does not prove that comparison.

The expanded revision develops the operator-domain and cone calculations, quotient derivative estimates, two-stratum construction, and cohomological compatibility in greater detail. The theorem hypotheses and conditional representation boundary are unchanged.

## Build and reproduce

Run `latexmk -pdf main.tex` in this directory. The descriptive PDF is the released manuscript; rebuilding writes `main.pdf`.

Run `python3 code/ih_llv_test.py` and `python3 code/t4_restricted_character.py`. These programs use exact arithmetic and Python's standard library. They reproduce the character table, dimension, Betti specialization and corank-one restriction, including failure controls. Expected transcripts are in `code/output/`; elapsed times may vary. See [the calculation guide](code/README.md).
