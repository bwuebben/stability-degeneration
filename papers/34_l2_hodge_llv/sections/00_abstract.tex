\begin{abstract}
We identify the Looijenga--Lunts--Verbitsky algebra on the intersection
cohomology of singular moduli spaces of sheaves on K3 surfaces
with the orthogonal Lie algebra of their second cohomology enlarged by
a hyperbolic plane. The surfaces have Picard rank one, the Mukai
vector is twice a primitive vector of square at least four, and
the rank and polarisation satisfy numerical conditions ensuring
that all semistable sheaves are locally free. These moduli spaces
have two singular strata and
admit no symplectic resolution. For their natural hyperk\"ahler
metric, we identify square integrable cohomology with intersection
cohomology and prove that harmonic forms are preserved by the three
Lefschetz operators and their adjoints. The analytic argument extends
the K\"ahler cone theorem to singular links and establishes invariance
of operator domains under metric comparisons that distinguish
tangential and normal directions at the strata. Monodromy then
extends the relations supplied by one positive three-plane to the
full second cohomology. We also determine the subrepresentation
generated by the unit and, under an explicit Hodge-theoretic
comparison hypothesis, the complete representation in dimension
eighteen.
\end{abstract}
