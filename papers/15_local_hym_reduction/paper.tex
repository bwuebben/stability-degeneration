\begin{abstract}
We give a quantitative local model for Hermitian--Einstein metrics near a
fixed locally free polystable bundle on a compact K\"ahler manifold.
Retaining the full convergent Kuranishi obstruction, we compare every small
endomorphism Dolbeault eigenvalue with the commutator form of the automorphism
action on harmonic deformations, and the harmonic metric on smooth pieces
of the reduced moduli space with the flat quotient metric. The model
reconstructs every sufficiently nearby Hermitian--Einstein solution.
The comparisons are relative and uniform through stabilizer changes and
arbitrary separation of positive eigenvalues of the finite form.
Canonical harmonic coordinates give quadratic error at fixed polarization,
with the same order for varying polarizations in moving harmonic backgrounds.
The proof derives the geometric estimates for finite balancing and
complementary elliptic reduction from orthogonality of the quadratic
reconstruction and gauge terms. We construct a stable rank-two bundle on a
smooth bicubic Calabi--Yau threefold with essential cubic deformation equations.
The theorem controls all nearby polystable deformations of the direct sum
of three copies, including families with arbitrarily separated small
positive eigenvalues.
\end{abstract}
