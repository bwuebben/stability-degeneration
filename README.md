# Stability walls and geometric degeneration

Twenty-two papers by [Bernd Johannes Wuebben](https://bwuebben.github.io/) connect stability chambers, small Dolbeault eigenvalues, Hermitian–Einstein metrics, integral topology and metric degeneration of compact K3 moduli, Hermitian–Yang–Mills flow, categorical phase flows, harmonic norm paths, a heterotic K3 compactification and Calabi–Yau conifold transitions.

## Reading the programme

Directories 01–04 contain the two-step foundations and their repeated-factor extension. Directories 05–07 develop three-step metrics and local moduli geometry; 08–09 form the dynamical pair. Directory 10 proves a general singular-graded obstruction on compact Kähler surfaces. Directory 11 studies mass increase and acyclic cancellation for phase flows on quiver complexes. Directory 12 constructs harmonic norm paths and labelled honeycombs from full boundary norms. Directory 13 applies the stationary and quotient-metric methods to local rank-three K3 bundle moduli. Directory 14 proves a general local harmonic/quiver metric comparison on projective K3 surfaces and applies it to a rank-five, two-parameter wall crossing. Directory 15 proves the general stationary local comparison on compact Kähler manifolds and constructs a stable bundle with essential cubic obstruction. Directory 16 proves uniform asymptotic phase and exponential relative metric tracking for confined HYM trajectories near arbitrary fixed locally free polystable bundles. Directory 17 constructs compact K3 moduli fourfolds with interacting exceptional cycles, integral attachments and actual harmonic collapse. Directory 18 proves general integral attachment and quantitative global metric results and realizes them in a compact rank-seven Grassmannian example. Directory 19 computes the full marked integral complement and all six attachment systems for the rank-three family, and proves the actual compact wall correspondences and normalized universal-class actions. Directory 20 identifies canonical Ricci-flat metrics and quantitative tangent cones on compact singular K3 bundle moduli without a symplectic resolution or a global smoothing. Directory 21 proves a uniform leading Yang–Mills scalar-scattering comparison across separated Higgs scales on an actual heterotic K3 family, with relative vector corrections, a selected curvature asymptotic and partial next-tree results. Directory 22 determines the small two-form spectrum for fixed polarized Calabi–Yau conifold smoothings, using exceptional periods and the global quotient harmonic norm; its compact applications connect integral topology to genuine smoothing geometry, including a family established in the Calabi–Yau smoothing programme. This numbering follows the development and mathematical dependencies of the programme and supplies a reading order; it is not part of the manuscript titles.

The bipartite spectral theorem and the toric chamber constructions give independent analytic and geometric starting points. The two-step metric-response paper joins them on the same tangent bundles. The repeated-factor spectral paper replaces scalar graph directions by matrix modes within its two-step class.

The three-step metric paper treats composable extensions with integrability relations and compares actual metrics and every small spectral scale with an independently balanced graph. The wall-moduli paper retains the full analytic obstruction and controls the harmonic L² metric on smooth pieces of the local quotient. The repeated-factor moduli paper develops the corresponding matrix estimates in a specified rank-five deformation problem. The K3 wall-crossing paper applies projection-relative balancing and complementary-gauge minimization to a complex twelve-dimensional bundle-moduli neighborhood. It compares actual harmonic metrics with cotangent-Grassmannian quotient metrics uniformly through stabilizer changes, giving collapse scales, complete rescaled limits and intrinsic distance asymptotics. This is a local application of the stationary and quotient-metric methods; global completion and bubbling remain outside its scope. The general K3 comparison retains all finite symmetry directions and allows arbitrary stabilizer changes, with linear spatial error unless a verified sign symmetry improves it to quadratic error. Its rank-five doubled-triangle example realizes five walls in two parameters. Intrinsic convergence through singular real levels yields complete rescaled limits under the stated convergence hypotheses, and selected transverse contractions have models T*P⁹, T*P¹¹ and T*P¹⁷. The general compact-Kähler local model retains the full convergent Kuranishi obstruction and proves uniform relative comparisons for actual HE metrics, every small endomorphism eigenvalue and harmonic quotient metrics at any fixed locally free polystable background. Its canonical harmonic coordinates give quadratic error, also for varying polarizations in moving harmonic backgrounds. A stable bundle on a smooth bicubic has essential cubic deformation equations; the result covers the full three-copy deformation problem. These are the stationary and moduli-geometric branches.

The compact K3 results connect integral topology and harmonic degeneration on the same exceptional cycles. In the fourfold family, a moving projective line specializes with multiplicity one to one line in each of two transverse Lagrangian planes. Their primitive classes have intersection matrix [[3,1],[1,3]] and an explicitly calculated attachment modulo eight; a single plane gives modulo three. Harmonic areas, volumes and ambient diameters remain controlled at unrelated positive scales, with complete rescaled limits and a common compact completion. The rank-seven eightfold has a deepest Gr(2,4) fibre. Both middle Schubert classes extend integrally, and its self-intersection six gives an attachment modulo six. A general graph-distortion theorem assembles whole-fibre quiver charts and principal-stratum paths into compact convergence under the stated no-bubbling and simple-zero hypotheses. The general additive reconstruction retains geometric overlaps and monodromy. For the rank-three fourfold family, the full marked complement ring, all six integral attachments and normalized universal-index actions are now calculated. The four corrected Mukai flops and two divisorial isomorphisms are identified geometrically; their cohomological loop is the identity. This global calculation uses the actual marked K3 and compact moduli problem.

For twice-primitive Mukai vectors on a Picard-rank-one K3 surface, a rank bound gives compact bundle moduli spaces with positive-dimensional singular strata and neither a symplectic resolution nor a global smoothing. Their harmonic L² metric equals the canonical singular Ricci-flat metric in a positive multiple of an ample determinant class. Exact quadraticity in harmonic Kuranishi coordinates yields holomorphic Ext-quiver models with relative O(r²) metric error, uniformly through stabilizer changes within each fixed chart. The regular-locus completion is the original space and its tangent cones are unique. The quartic example has dimension 18 and singular strata of dimensions 12 and 6.

For dynamics, the initial-condition paper proves differentiable phase selection relative to a chosen invariant graph, with uniform exponential tracking near a stable family. The wall-and-unstable paper proves confinement in moving coefficients even when diagonal metrics diverge, and identifies a residual carrying initial conditions. The general dynamical theorem retains arbitrary multiplicities, noncommuting automorphisms and the complete convergent Kuranishi obstruction. Under coefficient confinement and small normal displacement, it selects an exact finite trajectory with C¹ initial phase and exponentially vanishing relative metric error, independently of weak positive finite scales and stabilizer changes, even for unbounded reconstructing gauges. An obstructed bicubic application includes explicit noncommuting integrable coefficients and their commutator spectrum. The singular-graded paper proves a general rank-two surface obstruction: the squared norm on an equal-slope line decays strictly faster than a bounded-remainder extension of the categorical weight model would permit. The theorem includes nonreduced singularities and repeated line factors, with toric and K3 examples. Its stationary curvature theorem concerns the toric family.

The categorical phase-flow paper gives an explicit mass-increase example under the positivity-based phase convention, followed by cancellation from arbitrary positive metrics for invertible source differentials. For surjective source differentials and a large least initial acyclic singular value, its general mixed theorem allows arbitrary surviving tuples, unequal dimensions and fixed upper-half-plane charges; constants are uniform in arrow count, stabilizer and acyclic condition number with dimensions and norm bounds fixed. Relaxation follows separately in equal dimensions at charges −1+i and 1+i when the cohomology Kempf–Ness function has finite infimum. The balanced and Pauli cases are corollaries. Static nonscalar estimates and an explicit C2 limit with divergent Hölder Hessian seminorms complement the dynamics. Uniform final relaxation and general quotient geometry remain open.

The harmonic-path paper constructs a finite algebraically labelled path from every full split boundary triple in every finite rank for the specified A₂ representation, Novikov field, symmetric charges and radial area form. Equality uniqueness holds in the finite regular principal-velocity class with intrinsic first-order matching. General boundary continuity and a global Fukaya correspondence remain open.

These theorems retain their stated bundle, polarization and local-domain hypotheses. The stationary local comparison is available for arbitrary fixed smooth polystable backgrounds. General local dynamics now admits uniform phase and relative tracking under confinement. Compact harmonic completion and integral attachments are now established for the specified K3 bundle-moduli contractions without bubbling. Universal confinement, algebraic asymptotics of the corrected flow, positive-charge metric compactifications, reconstruction without supplied global overlap data, and a replacement asymptotic law for singular graded sheaves remain open. The moduli-space topology does not by itself reconstruct the underlying K3 surface or a general degenerating manifold. The surface obstruction does not contradict the weight-filtration theorem with locally free graded object.

## Build and reproduce

Each paper has a source and reproduction guide in its directory. Papers 01–03 and 05–10 have assembled, self-contained `paper.tex` files apart from their supplied figures. Papers 11–19 directly include their supplied sections and bibliography. Compile with `latexmk -pdf paper.tex`. Papers 20 and 21 use `latexmk -pdf main.tex`. The earlier repeated-factor spectral paper uses `main.tex` and `sh scripts/build.sh`.

Run `python3 reproduce.py` from the repository root to regenerate the finite computations and figures. Programs with third-party dependencies use `uv` and the paper's pinned `requirements.txt` or inline script dependency; the others use the Python standard library. The finite calculations establish the printed identities and examples, while the geometric, elliptic and parabolic assertions rely on the proofs. Released PDF hashes and archive identities are in `manuscripts.json`.

The heterotic K3 application resolves three leading vector masses with ratio-independent relative geometric error. Its defined first-order tree vector pencil includes the leading metric and two-form towers. The scalar interaction is a leading large-volume coefficient taken before the degeneration at fixed positive amplitude ratio; it does not establish exact string poles or a uniform finite-string-scale curvature remainder.

The Zenodo records preserve the released manuscripts. Papers 01–10 await arXiv submission. Papers 17–20 were released without an arXiv submission or queue entry. Paper 21 has a prepared source package; its arXiv submission remains pending.
